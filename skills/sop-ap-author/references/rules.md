## Governing principles

Lampiran Bab I.E, PDF pages 9-11, printed pages 4-6, specifies clarity, efficiency and effectiveness, alignment, measurable outputs, adaptability, user orientation, legal compliance and legal certainty. Execution requires consistency, commitment, continuous improvement, participation and documentation. Use these criteria to compare real alternatives rather than merely naming them in a draft.

Bab II.A, PDF pages 12-17, distinguishes technical and administrative procedures, macro and micro procedures, final and partial procedures, and generic and specific procedures. Do not impose technical machine-level instructions on an administrative handoff procedure. The source's explanatory examples do not establish a minimum or maximum number of steps for the user's SOP.

## Identification and naming

Bab III.C.1, PDF pages 51-53, printed pages 46-48, identifies SOPs through organizational duties and functions, actual activities and final outputs. Table 6 records No., Tugas, Fungsi, Uraian Tugas, Kegiatan, Output, Aspek Kegiatan and Judul SOP. Combine the activity aspect with the final output to form a title, as in "Penyusunan Renstra". Retain the distinction between a complete macro output and a partial micro output.

## Flowchart format

Bab II.B.2, PDF pages 20-22, printed pages 15-17, requires the vertical branching flowchart format for SOP AP, including short procedures with no decision. This does not require inserting artificial branches into a linear procedure.

| Symbol | Meaning | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kapsul / terminator | Start and end activities | PDF page 20, II.B.2.b.1 |
| Kotak / process | Execution activity | PDF page 21, II.B.2.b.2 |
| Belah ketupat / decision | Decision activity | PDF page 21, II.B.2.b.3 |
| Anak panah / arrow | Direction of the activities | PDF page 21, II.B.2.b.4 |
| Segilima / off-page connector | Connect symbols on different pages | PDF page 21, II.B.2.b.5 |

Only these five symbols belong to this format. The source does not use a same-page circle connector, document symbol, delay symbol or preparation symbol. Activity text belongs in the Kegiatan column, separate from actor columns and symbols. Decision explanations identify the outcomes. Figure 1 on PDF page 23 and Figure 4 on PDF page 30 show labeled return paths; vertical layout does not mean all arrows must go forward.

Bab II.B.2.c, PDF page 22, requires active verbs without an actor subject. Use the actor's role or jabatan in its separate column. Order actors by the activity sequence rather than institutional hierarchy, and start at the leftmost actor column. An activity can name a recipient when that information clarifies the handoff, for example "Mengirim surat undangan kepada peserta".

## Identity sheet

Bab II.C.2.a, PDF pages 27-29, printed pages 22-24, defines 13 identity elements:

| Element | Required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Logo and organization name | Actual nomenclature of the organization, unit or work unit |
| Nomor SOP AP | Number under the institution's applicable tata naskah dinas |
| Tanggal pembuatan | First completion date, not the date drafting began |
| Tanggal revisi | Revision date or planned review date |
| Tanggal efektif | Commencement date, or the signing date of the SOP document |
| Pengesahan | Competent official's job title, signature, name, NIP and institutional stamp |
| Judul SOP AP | Procedure within the unit's duties and functions |
| Dasar hukum | Regulations supporting this procedure and their implementing rules |
| Keterkaitan | Other standardized procedures directly related to the activity |
| Peringatan | Conditional risks, consequences, deadlines and remedies when needed |
| Kualifikasi pelaksana | Common competencies required to perform the procedure, not an individual's biography |
| Peralatan dan perlengkapan | Main tools and supplies directly needed by the procedure |
| Pencatatan dan pendataan | Records, forms and acknowledgments that demonstrate the work and handoff occurred |

The regulation does not prescribe a universal SOP number format. Do not derive dates, legal applicability or approval from a populated field alone. An empty revision field in an original draft is not an invitation to invent a revision event. Confirm the institution's convention.

## Mutu baku and procedure matrix

Bab II.C.2.b, PDF pages 29-30, printed pages 24-25, describes numbered activities, activity descriptions, actor columns, kelengkapan, waktu, output and keterangan. Figure 4 groups kelengkapan, waktu and output under Mutu Baku and shows Keterangan separately.

For each activity, state the actual required input or supporting resource, a duration in Waktu with its time unit, and a specific output that can be checked. Use applicable confirmed standards or representative observed times when available. Otherwise fill draft Waktu with a reasonable initial estimate and identify it as estimated. Represent handoffs and exception handling in activities and labeled routes. Use Keterangan only for necessary short context that does not fit the activity or another matrix field. Connect each output to the next activity's inputs. Do not equate an arithmetic sum of every row with elapsed service time when there are alternative branches, return loops, parallel work or waiting periods.

This skill defaults to provisional timings that can be revised from actual cases, unless the user explicitly chooses another drafting approach. A missing measurement or official standard alone is not a reason to leave Waktu empty. Base an estimate on the actual work and stated normal-case assumptions, and record whether it covers active work or waiting. For example, "Memeriksa kelengkapan berkas permohonan" may initially use "5 menit, estimasi awal untuk satu berkas yang siap diperiksa". That is an illustrative estimate, not a prescribed duration. Use a clear draft-wide label when all values are estimated, or identify the estimated rows when confirmed and estimated times are mixed. Keep the label and basis with the delivered draft. Compare actual case records with the initial values and adjust estimates during revision, retaining their basis. Preserve established standards and identify a proposed change separately. This estimation workflow is the skill's drafting default, not an added regulatory rule.

Under the skill's default of concise `Ket.`, turn a note such as "Berkas tidak lengkap dikembalikan kepada pemohon" into "Mengembalikan berkas yang belum lengkap kepada pemohon" in Kegiatan, assign the responsible actor and connect the correction route. Keep a short qualifier with its existing activity when no separate work is involved.

## Documentation and approval

Bab II.C.1, PDF pages 24-26, printed pages 19-21, covers a title page with organization and year, the competent leadership's adoption decision, a table of contents and a usage explanation containing scope, summary and definitions. The cover may follow institutional needs. These are parts of the documented SOP manual; a review of a single procedure sheet does not prove their presence in the broader manual.

Bab II.D, PDF pages 31-32, printed pages 26-27, describes hierarchical compilation and adoption. Bab III.C.4-5, PDF pages 54-55, printed pages 49-50, distinguishes simulation by the drafting team, trial with actual actors and approval by leadership. A machine-generated draft or lint pass does not carry out these institutional actions.

## Implementation and review

Bab III.D, PDF pages 55-57, printed pages 50-52, covers implementation planning, notification, distribution and accessibility, training and supervision.

Bab III.E, PDF pages 58-62, printed pages 53-57, calls for regular monitoring every six months as well as monitoring during execution, and regular evaluation within a one-year period plus evaluation as needed. Table 7 records the procedure, whether implementation runs well, evidence or reasons, action and assessor initials. Table 8 tests performance improvement, understanding, practical execution, role fulfillment, problem resolution, organizational performance needs and consistency between SOPs. Its marking convention is `X` for a statement not met and `-` for one met. Preserve that convention when reproducing the source form, or explain a clearly labeled alternative.

Propose review plans only when relevant to the user's request. Creating a skill or draft does not authorize setting reminders, contacting actors or submitting documents for approval.
